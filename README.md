# Cybersecurity Investigations Portfolio

Welcome to my cybersecurity investigation portfolio.

I'm a Bachelor of Information Technology student majoring in Cybersecurity at Southern Cross University, developing practical skills in cyber threat intelligence, digital forensics, penetration testing and security operations.

This repository documents my hands-on investigations, technical findings and ongoing development towards a career in cybercrime intelligence and digital investigations.

## Investigation Methodology

My investigations follow an **Attack → Detect → Investigate → Report** approach, combining offensive and defensive cybersecurity techniques.

Each investigation includes:

- **Objective:** What is being investigated and why
- **Environment:** Tools, systems and authorised lab environments
- **Methodology:** Investigative procedures and technical analysis
- **Evidence:** Logs, screenshots and technical findings
- **Analysis:** Interpretation of evidence and observed activity
- **MITRE ATT&CK:** Relevant adversary techniques, where applicable
- **Conclusion:** Findings and assessment
- **Recommendations:** Remediation or further investigative actions
- **Lessons Learned:** Skills developed and areas for improvement
